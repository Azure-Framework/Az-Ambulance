EMS Guide
Cleaner duty flow, shared dispatch, and on-screen rebinding.

1. Go On Duty

Toggle duty before using the EMS systems. Once on duty you will receive live dispatch updates and can join open calls.

2. Open Dispatch

Use the dispatch board to GPS, join, detach, close calls, force cancel bad incidents, and update your status. Other EMS can join the same call to assist.

3. Treat Patient

Use assessment, CPR, and stretcher flow on scene. Cardiac calls still require effective CPR before transport.

4. Transport

Load the patient, route to the nearest hospital, and complete the transport to clear the incident.
